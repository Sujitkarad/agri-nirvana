🚀 Excited to unveil **Agri Nirvana** — an Enterprise Precision Agriculture, AI Crop Care, AgFintech, and Market Linkage Platform designed to bring institutional-grade technology to millions of smallholder farmers with **100% Free Open Access**.

Over 60% of Indian farmers struggle with delayed crop disease detection, unorganized market price discovery, and climate volatility. **Agri Nirvana** bridges this gap by combining real-time computer vision, satellite telemetry, and conversational LLMs into a high-performance web application.

---

### 🌟 Key Technical Innovations & Architecture:

🌾 **Interactive 3D WebGL Visual Engine (Three.js)**:
- **Hero Crop Model**: Low-poly 3D wheat stalk with procedural Y-axis auto-rotation and dynamic dual-theme shaders.
- **3D Disease Leaf Inspector**: Curved 3D leaf geometry displaying glowing red/amber lesion overlays mapped to AI vision confidence levels, featuring 360° interactive drag inspection.
- **3D NDVI Terrain & Drone Flyover**: Sentinel-2 satellite vegetation index draped over a heightmap terrain mesh, featuring an autonomous 3D quadcopter drone scanner casting vertical laser beams.

🤖 **Dual AI Diagnostic Engine**:
- **Computer Vision**: ResNet-50 style neural vision model delivering leaf disease diagnosis & 3-tier treatment plans (organic/chemical/preventive) in under 50ms.
- **3D Animated Hugging Face Conversational Bot**: Integrated with `Mistral-7B` & `Llama-3.2` models, pre-processed Kaggle APMC vegetable price datasets, and multi-lingual Web Speech API audio advisory (Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Punjabi, English).

📡 **GIS Outbreak Radar & Micro-Climate Telemetry**:
- 25km radius GIS cluster radar with community outbreak logging to prevent regional disease contagion.
- Soil sensor dashboard tracking moisture, temperature, EC, and NPK depletion.

💳 **AgFintech & Market Linkage**:
- **Kisan Credit Scoring**: 0–100 weighted credit model + PMFBY Parametric Weather Drought Cover claim payout simulator.
- **e-NAM Market Linkage**: Live APMC Mandi price feeds and direct harvest listing portal connecting farmers with corporate buyers (*BigBasket, Sahyadri FPO, Reliance*).
- **Carbon ESG**: Resource conservation tracker (42,800+ L water saved) + 1-click printable Impact Certificate.
